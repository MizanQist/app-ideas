Agencies are consistently profitable, and small focused ones do best

The average digital agency made a 13% after-tax net margin in 2025 and about 15% on average since 2015. Agencies that narrowed their services earned 30%.

US marketing agencies average $163,000 revenue per employee. We plan far lower revenue per head, offset by far lower cost per head.

These are North American benchmarks from Promethean Research, the most-cited agency finance survey. Two lessons shape our plan: stay focused on a few services, and stay small and efficient. Our target operating margin of 47% is above these US averages because our costs are Nigerian while most revenue comes in pounds and dollars; even the half-speed case holds 41%.
