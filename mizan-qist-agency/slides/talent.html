Talent advantage

Nigeria ranks 6th in the world for outsourced talent

The 2026 Global Outsourcing Talent Index put Nigeria in the top 3% of 193 countries, ahead of several European economies.

The government's National Talent Export Programme targets 1 million export-linked jobs.

Talent supply is the other half of the thesis. Nigeria ranked sixth globally in the 2026 index. The rate comparison is for vetted senior contractors. One caution to raise ourselves: good Nigerian talent can also work remotely for foreign companies directly, so we raise pay as revenue grows and offer profit share to senior staff.
