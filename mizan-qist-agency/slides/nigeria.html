Nigeria is a large, under-served home market. Only 45.5% of people are online, but social media use grew by 12 million in a single year. The 39.7 million MSMEs figure is from the most recent SMEDAN/NBS survey; most are micro-businesses, so our Nigerian target is the upper tier: established SMEs, corporates and funded startups.
