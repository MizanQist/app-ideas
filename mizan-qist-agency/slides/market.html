A global market heading past $1 trillion

Global digital marketing spend was valued at $456.7 billion in 2025 and is forecast to reach $1.2 trillion by 2034, about 11% growth a year.

Nigeria's own online advertising and digital media market is valued at about $1.1 billion.

Sources: IMARC Group, Digital Marketing Market (2026); Research and Markets, Nigeria Online Advertising and Digital Media Market

Research firms size this market differently; estimates for the agency slice alone range from about $20bn to $385bn depending on definitions, so I've used the broad digital marketing figure from IMARC and say so if asked. The point for us isn't the headline; it's that the market is huge and growing about 11% a year, so a small agency needs a tiny share to thrive.
