The opportunity

The same digital work costs far less to deliver from Nigeria

$138/hr

Average self-reported hourly rate of a US digital marketing agency

£75–200

Typical hourly charge-out range at a UK marketing or creative agency

$32/hr

Average contract rate for a senior Nigerian developer, about half the North American rate

Clients in the UK and US pay for skills, not postcodes. An agency that sells at a fair international price and delivers from Abuja keeps the difference as margin.

Sources: Promethean Research 2026 via Haus Advisors; Move at Pace (UK, 2026); Lemon.io Nigeria rate report (2026)

The core thesis is price arbitrage with quality. US agencies average about $138 an hour; UK agencies charge £75 to £200. A senior Nigerian developer on contract averages $32.10 an hour, roughly 50% below North America and 25% below Europe/UK seniors. We don't need to undercut the market by much to win on price and still earn high margins.
